title: Use World, ScreenFrame and BackdropLights for a lit room
tags: room, world, scene, frames, lighting
---
World, ScreenFrame and BackdropLights from @toros/core give a lit room with framed screens. Use them if the installed version has them; otherwise build the room from meshes and lights.

In a four-section room or sphere the neighbouring screens are 90 degrees away, so only pillars and walls show the round shape. A flat single framed screen is the usual reason a passing site looks empty.
