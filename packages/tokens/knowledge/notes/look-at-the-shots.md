title: A score of 100 does not mean it looks good
tags: shots, quality, score, review, workflow
---
A trial site scored 100 out of 100 and still looked flat: one framed screen, near-black walls, no visible neighbours.

After the check passes, run toros shots with both viewports, open the contact sheet and compare it with the brief. Fix anything flat, empty, too dark, cut off or hard to read, then check again.
