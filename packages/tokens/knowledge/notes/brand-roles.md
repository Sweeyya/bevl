title: Brand roles for materials and lights
tags: brand, materials, lighting, roles, colour
---
BrandMaterial takes a role: primary, secondary, surface or accent. Never hard-code colours that contradict the brand.

A very dark surface stays almost black unless the scene has light. Add BrandLights or BackdropLights, then look at the shots. The emissive accent can render brighter than its token, so check it against the palette.
