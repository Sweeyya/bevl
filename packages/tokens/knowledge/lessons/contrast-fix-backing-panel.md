title: Fix low contrast with a backing panel, not black text
tags: contrast, text, html, a11y, brand

Mistake: The contrast fix suggested turning text black, which broke dark brands, and faded text was counted as failing.

Rule: Put the text on a solid brand-coloured backing panel that fits the phone width, and keep the brand text colour. Do not suggest a radius on the panel.
