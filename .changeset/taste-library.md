Toros now has its own taste for sites with no brand: a brief file (`toros.brief.json`) with validation, and a library of six original art directions that are dealt deterministically from the brief. New commands `toros brief validate`, `toros taste list` and `toros taste deal --apply`, MCP tools `validate_brief` and `deal_art_direction`, and a new check `brand/not-default-look`.
